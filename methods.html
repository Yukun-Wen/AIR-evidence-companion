How this library is built

  1. Corpus capture. All 247 works cited in the AIR manuscript are parsed from the bibliography and de-duplicated; each receives a stable ID (e.g. EMBRYO01).
  2. Section mapping. Every work is filed under the manuscript section that first cites it (primary section); additional supporting sections are recorded on the card.
  3. Claim extraction. Each manuscript sentence that cites a work is stored verbatim as a claim, highlighted yellow on the card and on the Claims page.
  4. Field extraction. For core empirical reports, evidence fields (task, inputs, methods, outcome measures, data split, validation, reproducibility) are extracted from the full text and shown in orange.
  5. Source locators. Excerpts are keyed to archived block locators (<ID>-Bxxxx) pinned to sha256 of the source text, so each paraphrase can be traced back.
  6. PDF mirroring. PDFs are mirrored only where a local copy existed or an open-access version was retrievable via OpenAlex / Unpaywall / Europe PMC; otherwise a DOI link is provided. No paywalled content is redistributed.
  7. Highlight annotation. For mirrored core reports, the passages whose content supports the manuscript claim are located in the PDF and marked with highlight annotations (highlight_pdfs.py).
