§ Design challenges and directions for validated AI — Transporting representations and evaluating their use
Transport tests characterize changes in centre, device, population, calendar period and clinical policy, together with any model adaptation. Calibration and discrimination describe how a probability model behaves under these changes, with uncertainty calculated at the independent sampling unit. Subgroup analyses can then examine probability accuracy, ranking, error burden, input access and consequences of use under explicit eligibility criteria. Implementation reviews identify advanced reproductive age as a priority population for this task-specific evaluation [@R002,R049].
