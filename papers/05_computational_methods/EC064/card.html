§ Computational methods — From cycle records to outcome prognosis
Table entry; table caption: Selected tabular prediction designs and evaluation conditions. — CNN over a reshaped tabular pseudo-image [@EC064]
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication. Human fresh-IVF EMR model comparison is directly in scope. The CNN transforms structured predictors into a pseudo-image; it does not analyze embryo images. Baseline, stimulation, retrieval, fertilization and embryo variables preclude calling this solely pretreatment prognosis. High reported results and denominator ambiguities do not establish ineligibility.
Method summary: Single-center fresh-cycle EMR data from 2009-2018 were mean-imputed or filtered for missingness, one-hot encoded, scaled, and randomly split 80:20. The authors compared random forest, decision tree, naive Bayes, a feedforward neural network, and a CNN that reshaped selected tabular predictors into a 6 by 7 pseudo-image. Five-fold cross-validation was used on training data, and SHAP/XGBoost-based feature importance was reported.
