§ Computational methods — Explicit events and recurrent trajectories
A ResNet18 annotation system converts frame probabilities into monotonic sequences through isotonic regression. This enforces developmental ordering, whereas an alternative postprocessing design permits reverse transitions. Annotation agreement evaluates the assigned stages; exploratory developmental clusters describe patterns in the resulting trajectories [@EE045,EE015].
Adjudicated scope: Learned automated annotation evaluated on human IVF embryo recordings is eligible core laboratory/embryo evidence; clinical utility is not required for technical eligibility.
Method summary: A ResNet18 frame classifier was trained from manually annotated material within a four-clinic dataset of 67,707 embryo videos. Frame-state probabilities were converted into monotonic whole-embryo state sequences by isotonic regression, validated on 1918 held-out embryos, and then used for K-means clustering of 14,159 embryos reaching at least eight cells by 66 hours.
