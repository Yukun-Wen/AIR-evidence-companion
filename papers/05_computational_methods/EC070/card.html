§ Computational methods — From cycle records to outcome prognosis
ANN/SVM live-birth models simulate transfer strategies through predictions conditional on historical treatment choices [@EC070]. A large historical ART analysis evaluates full-cycle and pre-cycle models using predominantly IVF and some donor-insemination records. Its narrative and model tables disagree on the processed denominator; repeated attempts also require patient-level accounting. Age associations are conditioned by the treatment-selection process represented in these records [@EC088].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication: human fresh IVF/ICSI cohort with directly developed ANN and SVM live-birth classifiers meets core scope. Regression-based screening does not make this a conventional-model-only report. Inputs include trigger-day, ovarian-response and transfer-strategy variables, so the applicable prediction time is within-cycle/transfer, not before treatment.
Method summary: Retrospective single-centre data from 1405 women were screened by univariate and multivariable regression to seven predictors, then used to train ANN and Gaussian-kernel SVM classifiers. Random internal splits were used, followed by a prospective-in-time-looking but same-centre application sample of 82 patients.
