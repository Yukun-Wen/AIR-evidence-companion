§ Computational methods — Detection: selecting the object of analysis
Original YOLO predicts boxes, confidence and conditional classes through a grid, with nearby objects testing its limited capacity. Later versions use different designs. Mask R-CNN adds instance masks to region-based detection and preserves coordinates through RoIAlign interpolation. Instance masks distinguish objects; semantic masks assign anatomical categories [@EM010,EM011].
