§ Computational methods — Explicit events and recurrent trajectories
Temporal constraints impose consistency on predicted stages. An earlier pipeline combines handcrafted and bag-of-features descriptors, local-temporal AdaBoost and Viterbi refinement. Overall frame accuracy improves, while three-cell accuracy remains 20.86%. Short-stage annotation therefore becomes a specific target for improving division-timing estimation [@EE083].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication: Complete conference study empirically evaluates learned classification of early human IVF embryo stages. Laboratory automation is core eligible although no clinical endpoint is evaluated.
Method summary: The three-level method combined 62 hand-crafted texture/shape features and 200 learned bag-of-features with per-frame and local-temporal AdaBoost classifiers, followed by Viterbi global refinement. Two experts annotated 716 human embryo videos from multiple clinical sites; 327 were used for training and 389 for testing.
