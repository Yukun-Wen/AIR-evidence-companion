§ Computational methods — End-to-end appearance models and transfer learning
Static-image ensembles also predict PGT-A labels. A multiclinic study uses model-based data cleansing during development and reports cleansed and uncleansed blind tests. Cleansing changes population composition and class prevalence. Keeping both results identifies performance in the filtered set and the broader unfiltered population [@EE059]. Vision Transformer encodes image patches with positional information and attention; its weaker image-specific inductive bias makes pretraining and scale consequential. Swin merges patches into hierarchical resolutions and links local windows through shifted partitions. Fixed-window attention scales linearly with token count [@EM017,EM018].
