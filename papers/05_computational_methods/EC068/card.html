§ Computational methods — From cycle records to outcome prognosis
Table entry; table caption: Selected tabular prediction designs and evaluation conditions. — XGBoost live-birth prediction in PCOS [@EC068]
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication. Retrospective learned live-birth prediction after fresh human IVF/ICSI transfer in PCOS with antagonist stimulation meets core scope. Transfer number/stage and trigger-day progesterone make this a late-treatment/transfer-stage prediction rather than baseline counseling. No external validation is reported.
Method summary: The single-center retrospective cohort included 1,062 PCOS antagonist-protocol fresh-transfer cycles from 2019-2023, randomly split 743/319. Missing data were handled with missForest after threshold-based exclusions. LASSO and recursive feature elimination yielded seven shared predictors, and seven algorithms were tuned with grid search and five-fold cross-validation. The held-out validation set was assessed with discrimination, classification, Brier score, calibration, decision curves, and SHAP.
