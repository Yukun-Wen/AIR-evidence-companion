§ Computational methods — Comparing temporal support and information paths
A MobileNetV2 study compares developmental image windows for outcome prediction using cycle-grouped splits. This design directly examines the value of observations acquired at different developmental stages. Its methods and discussion assign miscarriage and biochemical pregnancy inconsistently, leaving the negative-class definition ambiguous for comparison with other live-birth models [@EE027].
Adjudicated scope: Human fresh-ICSI transferred-embryo development-window and live-birth classifier study meets core eligibility despite unresolved negative-label wording.
Method summary: The single-centre study used 700 fresh ICSI transfer videos, comprising 443 successful and 257 unsuccessful embryos. MobileNetV2 transfer learning was applied to manually selected stage-specific frames, with cycle-grouped train/test allocation for outcome models, repeated training, and five-fold cross-validation. Live-birth prediction was evaluated across pronuclear, cleavage, morula-adjacent, and blastocyst time points.
