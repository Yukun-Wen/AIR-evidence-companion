§ Computational methods — Early, intermediate and late fusion
Uterine imaging provides complementary fusion designs. An endometrial study clusters ultrasound regions into habitats, derives radiomic features and combines them with clinical variables for clinical-pregnancy prediction. This structure introduces imaging information and regional organization as two components. Comparisons with clinical-only and whole-region models can separate their contributions. A prospective first-IVF/ICSI cohort uses expert MUSA ultrasound features in a pretreatment cumulative-live-birth model; adding embryo stage and frozen-transfer information post hoc yields a later-information predictor [@EC057,EC058].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication: This prospective human first-IVF/ICSI cohort evaluates XGBoost prediction of cumulative live birth using pretreatment ovarian reserve, uterine ultrasound and symptom features. The direct clinical endpoint meets core eligibility despite modest model performance.
Method summary: A cohort of 1,037 women undergoing their first IVF/ICSI treatment received standardized expert 2D/3D transvaginal ultrasound before ART. XGBoost was trained with an 80/20 stratified random split and five-fold cross-validation/Optuna tuning; low-frequency variables were removed and SHAP guided interpretation. The primary model used pretreatment variables; embryo stage and frozen transfer were added only post hoc.
