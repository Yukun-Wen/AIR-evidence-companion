§ Computational methods — End-to-end appearance models and transfer learning
An eight-network ResNet/DenseNet ensemble processes whole and zona-masked blastocyst images. Blind evaluation combines contributing-clinic and unseen-clinic datasets, while embryologist comparisons use a graded subset and converted morphology thresholds. Representation ensembling and comparator construction both affect the reported contrast [@EE076].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication: Empirical deep-learning evaluation of human Day-5 embryo images with fetal-heart outcomes is eligible. Two pivotal test sets are clinic-disjoint, while the combined blind set includes same-clinic testing.
Method summary: Retrospective data comprised 8,886 embryo images from 11 clinics in three countries. A pilot single-clinic phase was followed by a pivotal multiclinic phase; the final ensemble contained eight ResNet-152/DenseNet-161 models using full-embryo or zona-masked inputs. Validation guided model selection, while three blind pivotal test sets included two sets from clinics absent from training.
