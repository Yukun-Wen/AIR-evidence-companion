§ Computational methods — Direct video encoders and shared representations
The later iDAScore development study uses separate pathways for early and blastocyst-stage transfer days and treatment-level splitting. Its multicenter dataset supports internal evaluation. The v1 comparator had previously trained on some v2 test samples, giving the compared versions different exposure histories on the evaluation set [@EE047].
Adjudicated scope: Multicenter learned human IVF embryo-ranking development and internal evaluation directly meet core criteria.
Method summary: The retrospective cohort began with 249,635 embryos from 34,620 treatments at 22 clinics; after exclusions, 181,428 embryos remained, including 33,687 transferred embryos with fetal-heartbeat outcomes and 147,741 clinically discarded embryos. Separate 3D CNN pathways handled day 2/3 and day 5+ images, with a direct-cleavage component and day-specific calibration; splitting occurred at treatment level.
