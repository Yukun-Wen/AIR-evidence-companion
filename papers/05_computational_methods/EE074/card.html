§ Computational methods — Explicit events and recurrent trajectories
An earlier multilayer-perceptron study adds manually measured expansion and trophectoderm dynamics to conventional timings. The internal comparison evaluates this richer feature set for fetal-heart implantation in donor-oocyte recipients. Missing measurements require preprocessing, linking the representation's biological detail to its practical availability [@EE074].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication: Human donor-oocyte ICSI single-transfer cohort with empirical learned ANN development meets core scope. Retrospective and selected-population limitations reduce inference, not eligibility.
Method summary: Single-center retrospective cohort of 637 fresh single-blastocyst transfers, with 451 embryos retained after preprocessing for ANN development. Five manually annotated novel time-lapse features and conventional morphokinetic timings were analyzed; four multilayer-perceptron models used an 85/15 learning/test split and five-fold cross-validation within the learning data.
