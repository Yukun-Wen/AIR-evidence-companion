§ Computational methods — Segmentation: constructing anatomical intermediates
Oocyte maturity classification can use explicit anatomical intermediates. One system passes DeepLabV3Plus germinal-vesicle/polar-body masks to a SqueezeNet-inspired classifier refined by a genetic algorithm. A related study compares 71 segmentation variants and identifies poor coverage of rare small structures. Both reports contain sample-count inconsistencies and leave patient isolation unspecified. The maturity report also gives a nonstandard accuracy equation, making its stated metric definition part of the result [@EC026,EC027].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication: The empirical two-network system classifies maturity of human oocytes collected from ICSI patients and explicitly supports the ICSI procedure. Core eligibility is clear, although metric definitions and split arithmetic require strict limits on numerical interpretation.
Method summary: DeepLabV3Plus segmented germinal-vesicle and first-polar-body regions, followed by a SqueezeNet-inspired classifier refined with a genetic algorithm. Stratified repeated random subsampling generated training, validation and test image sets; augmentation and balanced batches addressed pronounced MI/MII/PI class imbalance.
