Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication. Comparative learned-model development in human autologous single-euploid frozen transfer qualifies even though LR wins. Explicit patient-based repeated partitioning and random intercepts address repeated transfers, unlike ambiguous cycle-only splitting. Restriction to PGT-A euploid D5-D7 embryos and transfer-stage information bounds applicability.
Method summary: The study analyzed 1,979 single-euploid FET cycles from 1,459 couples treated during 2017-2023. Mixed-effects logistic regression used patient random intercepts for repeated cycles. LR, random forest, SVM, and XGBoost were evaluated with 2,000 repeated 60:40 patient-level splits, followed by static four-fold cross-validation of the selected LR model. Candidate inputs included BMI, endometrial preparation and thickness, embryo morphology, biopsy day, and clinical history.
