§ Computational methods — Trajectory summaries and recorded-action learning
Action imitation learns recorded decisions. A LightGBM system predicts an expert's retrieval and medication choices from monitoring visits, including low-dose hCG for supplementation of LH activity. This target differs from trigger prescribing, and drug-specific agreement differs from complete-prescription agreement. Another ANN/SVR dose model duplicates records according to proximity of realized yield to a target before splitting the expanded rows, creating possible cross-partition copies unless grouped. Its later same-centre test evaluates agreement with clinician doses. The resulting objective is outcome-weighted prescribing imitation [@EC039,EC041].
Adjudicated scope: Human IVF/ICSI first-cycle data empirically train ANN/SVR for initial gonadotropin dose, so the work meets learned clinical-decision scope. Outcome-weighted imitation and serious validation risks do not erase eligibility; they sharply limit performance and benefit claims.
Method summary: Prediction time is before stimulation at selection of the gonadotropin starting dose. Unit: 1,555 first-cycle patients for development and 81 subsequent patients for application. Endpoint transformation: the direct target was the historical clinician-assigned starting dose, while records were outcome-weighted according to closeness of retrieved-oocyte count to 16; rounded weights duplicated 1,555 original records into 4,037 rows. Missing-data handling is not reported in the main text and cannot be determined. ANN used random 70/17/13 training/validation/test partitions of the duplicated rows; SVR used 80/20. Comparator was SVR versus ANN, and later application compared recommendations with clinician doses. This primarily measures prescriptive agreement with historical clinician practice. The weighting uses observed outcomes, but no randomized or prospective counterfactual comparison establishes that the recommended dose causally improves oocyte yield or clinical outcomes.

EC041-B0006 The development cohort comprised 1,555 women undergoing their first IVF/ICSI oocyte-retrieval cycle at one center.
EC041-B0022 Outcome-based weighting duplicated each original patient record up to a rounded weight, expanding 1,555 patients into 4,037 modeling rows before splitting.

EC041-B0057 In 81 later patients, model-recommended dose differed from clinician dose by a mean absolute 14.08 IU.
