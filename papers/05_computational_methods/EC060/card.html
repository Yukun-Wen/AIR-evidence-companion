§ Computational methods — From cycle records to outcome prognosis
Table entry; table caption: Selected tabular prediction designs and evaluation conditions. — Baseline and treatment-phase clinical-pregnancy classifiers [@EC060]
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication. Original learned models for human IVF/ICSI clinical pregnancy meet scope. Pretreatment and treatment-phase inputs represent distinct decision times, but both are developed in selected fresh cycles with a good-quality embryo and exclude several early treatment failures. Last cycle per woman is selected; later validation remains at the same center.
Method summary: The authors selected only the last recorded cycle for women with multiple IVF/ICSI cycles, producing 1,062 women treated in 2016-2021 and a 250-patient 2022 temporal cohort. They used training/internal-validation splitting, KNN imputation, regression and LASSO screening, randomized hyperparameter search with ten-fold cross-validation, and compared LR, XGBoost, random forest, SVM, naive Bayes, and LightGBM. Model 1 used baseline predictors; Model 2 added stimulation and embryo-development information available before transfer.
