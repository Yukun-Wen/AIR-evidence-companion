§ Review methodology — Review design
12 This methods-centered survey combines computational explanation with a scoping-review protocol and source-located evidence synthesis. It examines how data structure shapes IVF and ICSI models, defines conditions for comparing their evaluations, and connects reported results to clinical uses. The reporting framework combines PRISMA-ScR, PRISMA-S and an adapted PRISMA 2020 flow [@PRISMASCR,PRISMAS,PRISMA2020]. Performance is synthesized within task, endpoint and evaluation setting.
