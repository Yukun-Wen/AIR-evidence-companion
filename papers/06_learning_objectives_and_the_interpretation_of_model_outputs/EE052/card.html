§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Genetic classification and assay-defined populations
A multiclass model of aneuploidy and mosaicism includes testing technique among the predictors of mosaicism, making assay practice part of the learned classification. Its confusion matrices and adjacent accuracy summaries disagree. This example links assay-specific predictors to two levels of interpretation: the genetic class being learned and the contingency table used to measure agreement with it [@EE052].
Adjudicated scope: Human IVF chromosomal-status machine-learning development and internal evaluation satisfy core scope despite unresolved performance reporting problems.
Method summary: The retrospective dataset comprised 6989 biopsied day-5 to day-7 blastocysts from 2476 IVF cycles between 2013 and 2020. Sixteen supervised classifiers were compared for four-class aneuploidy and mosaicism outcomes, with class balancing, preprocessing, cross-validation during selection, and final Random Forest evaluation using out-of-bag predictions.
