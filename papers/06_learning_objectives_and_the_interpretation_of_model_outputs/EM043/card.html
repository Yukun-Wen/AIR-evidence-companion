§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Probability estimation, discrimination and calibration
Proper scoring rules reward truthful forecast distributions in expectation; calibration evaluates fitted predictions. Kendall and Gal combine dropout-based parameter uncertainty with heteroscedastic observation-noise models in vision, obtaining formulation- and metric-dependent calibration. IVF abstention policies can assess such estimates through coverage, error and clinical outcomes [@EM031,EM043].
