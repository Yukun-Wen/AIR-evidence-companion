§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Policy objectives and clinical utility
Decision-curve analysis evaluates model-based decisions at specified benefit--harm trade-offs. Vickers and Elkin weight false-positive decisions by threshold odds when computing net benefit and compare a model or binary rule with relevant default strategies. The threshold links predicted risk to an action's consequences, supplying a model-based complement to direct clinical evaluation [@EM030].
