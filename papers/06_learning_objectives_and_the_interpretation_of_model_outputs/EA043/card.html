§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Sequential outcomes and transfers until live birth
Cluster-specific support-vector models estimate cumulative pregnancy from baseline characteristics. Internal patient-split evaluation reports modest discrimination and errors in cluster-level outcome curves, with comparator performance varying across analyses. The curve errors describe group-level fit for pregnancy over the specified horizon [@EA043].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication. Human IVF cohort modelling with learned clustering and SVM-based predictors directly addresses cumulative pregnancy prediction from pretreatment characteristics and meets the frozen empirical learned-method criteria.
Method summary: The single-center retrospective cohort included 11,190 Chinese couples treated from 2016 to 2018. Logistic regression on first-cycle outcomes selected 11 baseline variables. Three approaches were compared: k-means clusters merged using log-rank tests, a probabilistic RBF support-vector machine, and a cluster-specific SVM. Two-thirds/one-third random splits were repeated 30 times, with AUC for cycles one to three and RMSE of cluster-level cumulative pregnancy curves.
