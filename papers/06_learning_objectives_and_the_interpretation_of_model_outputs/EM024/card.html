§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Sequential outcomes and transfers until live birth
DeepSurv extends Cox partial likelihood with nonlinear log-risk estimation and evaluates event-time ordering through concordance. Its non-IVF studies include an RCT-derived test dataset and a secondary analysis of agreement between treatment recommendations and outcomes. The former supplies randomized treatment data; the latter evaluates the learned recommendations retrospectively [@EM024].
