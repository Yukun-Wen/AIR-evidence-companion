§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Genetic classification and assay-defined populations
Mosaic handling determines an important part of the class definition. A model combining multi-focus temporal features and clinical variables groups mosaicism below 50% with euploid embryos and retrains its PGT-SR subgroup internally. It reports patient splitting alongside conflicting analyzed-video counts. Its class definition differs from the federated study's non-aneuploid category, making the mosaic rule an explicit field for harmonizing results [@EE041,EE030].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication: Human IVF/PGT temporal-image and clinical-feature models meet core scope, including retrained PGT-SR subgroup.
Method summary: The single-center retrospective study collected 368 PGT cycles from 355 couples. AMSNet combined ResNet50, multi-focus attention, and temporal shifting for real-time blastocyst prediction. AMCFNet fused embryo video features with selected clinical variables for euploidy classification. Patients were split 60/20/20 for the overall tasks; the PGT-SR subgroup was retrained on 80% and tested on 20%. PGT-A/PGT-SR provided the reference labels.
