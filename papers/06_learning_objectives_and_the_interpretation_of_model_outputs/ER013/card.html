§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Measurement: spatial and ordinal agreement
An empty union requires a scoring or exclusion convention. Overlap evaluates labeled structure; object counts and thickness errors assess other properties. The metric-pitfalls taxonomy separates problem-category mismatch, metric selection and application errors. Metrics Reloaded converts these distinctions into problem-specific choices, including sperm motility and embryo segmentation, with biological hierarchy and aggregation specified [@EM038,EM037]. A CASA-versus-manual systematic review compares semen parameters and control-bead experiments, providing a procedure-agreement framework for laboratory validation [@ER013].
