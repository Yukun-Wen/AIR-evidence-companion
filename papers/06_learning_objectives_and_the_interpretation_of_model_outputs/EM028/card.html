§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Probability estimation, discrimination and calibration
Ranking and probability calibration capture different properties of a score. Calibration-in-the-large, slope and flexible curves characterize agreement between predictions and observed frequencies. Temperature scaling adjusts neural logits after fitting, while clinical calibration frameworks organize evaluation and updating. Assessing these properties across clinics identifies the recalibration needed for a new population [@EM028,EM029].
