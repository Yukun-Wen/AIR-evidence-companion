## Source-linked excerpts
- [EE067-H0125] ==The experiment used 200 pairs of Day-5 euploid embryos with known outcomes and asked embryologists to choose before and after AI identification of the higher-ranked image.==
- [EE067-H0151] ==The same sets were repeated one year later and then again seven days later with reordered images and AI highlighting, creating a pre-post repeated-exposure design.==
- [EE067-H0225] ==The AI selected the implanted embryo in 78.5% of pairs and embryologists improved from 65.5% to 73.6% after seeing the AI choice.==
- [EE067-H0245] ==At least 75% embryologist consensus occurred in 62% of pairs before AI and 74% after AI.==
- [EE067-H0258] ==The authors identify retrospective design, biopsy effects, uterine confounding, and a small embryologist sample as limitations and call for prospective randomized validation.==
