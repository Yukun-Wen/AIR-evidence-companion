§ Quantitative evidence and supported decision claims — Decision benefit requires evaluation of a clinical policy
A retrospective iDAScore/KIDScore comparison evaluates scores already used in transfer selection, with most transfers containing multiple embryos. Cycle-level birth attaches the observed outcome to the transferred group, and score-informed selection defines which embryos contribute that outcome. The report's adjusted coefficients, odds ratios and intervals are internally inconsistent, leaving the adjusted association uncertain [@EE037].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication: Human IVF evaluation of learned iDAScore against KIDScore is eligible despite major reporting and outcome-attribution limitations.
Method summary: The single-center study included 91 IVF/ICSI cycles and 429 blastocysts. Embryos received KIDScore D5 and iDAScore v1.2.0 ratings, with high-scoring embryos prioritized for transfer. Most transfers used two embryos and some used three. Cycle-level live birth was related to average scores using logistic regressions, adjustment for maternal age and blastocyst number, paired comparisons, and ROC analysis.
