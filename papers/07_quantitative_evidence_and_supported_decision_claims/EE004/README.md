> ==Observational human-AI studies characterize how scores enter clinical decisions. A ranking-concordance study relates embryologist experience and cohort size to agreement with a 3D-CNN score. MAIA prospectively evaluates a static-image ANN during routine multicentre single-embryo transfer, including cases selected according to AI despite embryologist disagreement. ## Source-linked excerpts
- [[EE004-B0009]] ==The retrospective single-centre cohort comprised PGT-A cycles cultured in EmbryoScope+ from February 2020 to October 2023; selection for transfer used morphology and euploid status while embryologists were blinded to AI scores.==
- [[EE004-B0013]] ==iDAScore v2 is a proprietary 3D convolutional neural network using 128 images from 20 to 148 hours post-insemination to output a 1.0-9.9 score associated with fetal-heartbeat likelihood.==
- [[EE004-B0018]] ==The final analysis included 1,321 treatments from 1,165 patients, 19,962 embryos and 20 embryologists across three experience categories.==
- [[EE004-B0020]] ==Among 1,321 transferred single euploid blastocysts, the score's fetal-heartbeat discrimination was AUC 0.669 (95% CI 0.637-0.700).==
- [[EE004-B0021]] ==Overall manual-AI top-choice concordance was 58.6% and rose from 51.3% for junior to 63.4% for Senior+ embryologists; adjusted contrasts were significant for Senior+ versus the other two groups.==
- [[EE004-B0032]] ==The authors identify PGT-A restriction, one site, unmeasured laboratory tasks and human factors, sex-selection effects, nonstandard experience categories, changing experience over time and strong confounding by cohort size as limitations.==
