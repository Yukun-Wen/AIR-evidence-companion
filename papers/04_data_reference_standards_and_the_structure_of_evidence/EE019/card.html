§ Computational methods — Segmentation: constructing anatomical intermediates
Blastocyst masks can generate area trajectories for formation-time estimation. A comparison of U-Net, HRNet and related segmenters includes a small private-clinic test, where compartment performance falls relative to the public-image comparison. Its results and conclusion give conflicting timing-error summaries; segmentation overlap and event-timing error require separate records [@EE019].
Adjudicated scope: Empirical human-IVF blastocyst segmentation and formation-time estimation is eligible technical laboratory evidence; private-clinic testing provides limited transportability evidence.
Method summary: The study trained U-Net, HRNet, DeepLab, and replicated RDU-Net models on 249 expert-annotated public blastocyst images, using cross-validation and an 85/15 split. Generalization was evaluated on 25 expert-annotated images from a different clinic. HRNet-derived trophectoderm and inner-cell-mass area trajectories were then used to estimate blastocyst formation time in 69 time-lapse videos.
