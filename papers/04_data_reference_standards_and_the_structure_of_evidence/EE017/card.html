§ Computational methods — Direct video encoders and shared representations
Another pipeline combines YOLO cropping, SimCLR image pretraining, LSTM sequence encoding and an XGBoost pregnancy predictor. Its external tests evaluate stage encoding; cross-validation evaluates the downstream pregnancy classifier in selected good-quality transferred embryos. The staged design permits representation quality and outcome discrimination to be analyzed at their respective evaluation levels [@EE017].
Adjudicated scope: Human transferred-embryo clinical-pregnancy prediction with learned time-lapse representations is eligible regardless of modest discrimination.
Method summary: At one French centre, 1,580 videos from 460 patients were used for self-supervised pretraining, with 209 embryos from 62 patients used for Siamese-network fine-tuning and 620 embryos from 312 patients assigned to validation. A YOLO preprocessing stage localized embryos, SimCLR learned static features, an LSTM encoded sequences, and XGBoost predicted clinical pregnancy. Validation separated patients and examined both matched-embryo and one-embryo-per-cycle tasks.
