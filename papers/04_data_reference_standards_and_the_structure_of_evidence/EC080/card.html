§ Computational methods — Morphometry and complementary image representations
Image ensembles combine learned representations. EfficientNetV2 feature and decision fusion addresses imbalanced, eighteen-class stained-sperm morphology in a reused image resource. SwinMobile-AE combines Swin Transformer and MobileNetV3 features through an autoencoder for sperm/impurity and morphology benchmarks. It fits separate models on each resource and augments test images, making the source-image unit central to evaluation. Both designs assess image classification within the acquisition conditions and resource-specific partitions [@EC078,EC080].
