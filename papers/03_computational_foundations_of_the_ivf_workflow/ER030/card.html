§ Computational foundations of the IVF workflow — Information, prediction units and computational tasks
A task is specified by its observation window, modality, prediction unit, target, intended action and evaluation setting. These terms also determine the denominator of a result. The 2017 international glossary and its update titled 2025, published in 2026, distinguish individual newborns from delivery events and require explicit denominators. This terminology history guides comparison while preserving the definition used by each original study [@ER029,ER030].
§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Probability estimation, discrimination and calibration
Outcome harmonization links each definition to its denominator and purpose. The Vienna laboratory consensus defines process indicators with reference populations, including injected MII oocytes for normal ICSI fertilization. COMMIT establishes a minimum outcome set and companion reporting definitions for infertility trials, including randomized-participant denominators where appropriate. Its live-birth threshold differs from the international glossary threshold, so extraction retains the definition used in each study. Twins contribute two newborns to a newborn count and one event to a delivery count [@ER033,ER034,ER035,ER030].
