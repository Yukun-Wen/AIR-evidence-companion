where the superscript denotes the outcome under a specified selection policy. Prediction studies characterize associations under observed care; policy evaluation compares outcomes under alternative decision processes. Allocation design and follow-up determine how the observed comparison identifies the effect of changing actions. Set-valued policy learning returns a set of candidate doses. Its IVF application models follicular-yield and estradiol trade-offs under causal and noisy-label assumptions. Coverage guarantees are evaluated using synthetic data, while the IVF analysis estimates the two laboratory targets under candidate dosing strategies [@EA003].
Adjudicated scope: AI adjudication. This full preprint has a real human-IVF ovarian-stimulation application of learned treatment policies to 18,538 multicentre cycles in addition to synthetic experiments. Direct IVF evaluation meets core scope; observational policy estimates do not prove causal benefit.
Method summary: The paper defines set-valued policies that return candidate treatment sets and proposes two approaches: a greatest-lower-bound construction from conditional-outcome confidence bounds, and conformal policy learning based on estimated optimal-treatment labels with controlled random-label injection. Synthetic experiments assess coverage, set size, and set-policy value. The IVF application uses multi-arm causal forests for label generation, a Super Learner outcome model, six ordinal gonadotropin-dose levels, follicular yield, and estradiol as an adverse-event-related secondary outcome.
