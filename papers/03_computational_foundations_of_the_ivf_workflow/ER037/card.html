§ Learning objectives and the interpretation of model outputs — Sequential outcomes and transfers until live birth
Sequential prediction defines an outcome over a treatment horizon. A discrete-time logistic model estimates first live birth across complete IVF cycles, each comprising fresh and frozen transfers from one stimulation. Its registry analysis provides an internally validated formulation whose estimates depend on the assumptions used for treatment discontinuation [@ER037].
