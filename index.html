About this resource

This Evidence Companion is a supplementary, reviewer-facing appendix to the manuscript Artificial intelligence for in vitro fertilization (submitted to Artificial Intelligence Review). Rather than asking a reviewer to take each of the 247 citations on trust, it makes the evidentiary chain inspectable at the level of individual sentences: every cited work is filed under the manuscript section that first invokes it, the verbatim claim passage it supports is highlighted, the evidence fields are machine-extracted, and each excerpt is keyed to a sha256-pinned locator in the archived full text. Where a PDF may be redistributed (local copy or open access), the passages supporting the claim are highlighted in situ.

Coverage. 247 cited works — 125 core empirical reports, 122 contextual sources — across 10 manuscript sections and 7 workflow groups; 20 conditional within-report comparisons; 91 mirrored PDFs (58 with in-situ highlight marks).

Status. All machine-derived content is flagged human verification pending; the author confirms each card before submission. Cite the manuscript itself when referencing this resource. Built with build_site.py; pipeline documented on the Methods page.
